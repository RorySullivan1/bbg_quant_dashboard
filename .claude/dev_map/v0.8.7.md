## 1. Summary & non-goals

The shipped v0.8.5 Regime Analysis section is structure-first: a pill-tabbed
Risk-vs-Return / Correlation pair with Volatility (4 VIX buckets) wired and
Trend / Liquidity / Rate-level inert. v0.8.7 finishes it:

1. **Drop the Correlation sub-tab** — correlation already lives in the
   Multi-Strategy tab (`regime_corr_matrix`); the Platform section becomes a
   single regime-conditioned risk/return scatter. Removes
   `_regime_heatmap`/`_update_regime_heatmap`, `regime_correlation`, the theme
   dropdown, and the pill sub-tab bar.
2. **Volatility:** drop the `VIX ≥ 35` bucket and uncap the second-highest →
   `VIX < 15` / `15 ≤ VIX < 25` / `VIX ≥ 25`.
3. **Trend:** terciles (low / middle / high thirds) of a selected benchmark's
   **21-day rolling return autocorrelation** (`rolling_autocorr`), with a
   conditional **benchmark** dropdown.
4. **Rate-level:** terciles of a selected **regional risk-free rate level**
   (`FEDL01` / `EONIA` / `MUTKCALM`), with a conditional **region** dropdown.
5. **Risk regime (new):** terciles of the daily **first-difference (Δ)** of
   `NFCIRISK Index` (robust near its zero crossings, unlike a raw pct-change).
6. **Drop the Liquidity** scaffold entirely.

Tercile bounds are derived live from the indicator's 1/3 & 2/3 quantiles over the
lookback window (`tercile_bounds`); the existing `regime_mask` /
`regime_risk_return` / `_update_regime_scatter` path is reused unchanged.

**Non-goals (hard constraints):**

- **Whole-catalog (ARP) only** — never the selected subset.
- **One BQL call per session unchanged** — the new indicators (`NFCIRISK`, the
  three regional rates) ride the single startup fetch via `REGIME_TICKERS` and
  are dropped from ARP views by the existing `reindex(columns=meta["ticker"])`.
- **No new runtime dependencies; no second BQL call.** All conditioning is a live
  re-slice of the cache.
- **Multi-Strategy tab untouched** (its Correlation heatmap + `regime_corr_matrix`
  stay); the factor scatter + sunburst are unchanged.
